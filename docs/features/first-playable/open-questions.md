# First playable product decisions

All material first-playable questions were resolved and approved by the owner on 2026-07-20. No product decisions block planning.

## 1. Multiplayer result ownership

Each player runs an independent round in the same arena. One player's success, death, timeout, disconnect, or reset does not end or change another player's round.

## 2. Safe-zone qualification

Success occurs immediately after the server confirms that the player entered the safe zone. No dwell duration applies.

## 3. Late-join policy

A late joiner spawns safely and starts exactly one independent round immediately without affecting existing players.
