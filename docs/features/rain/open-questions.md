# Cosmetic rain product decisions

All material cosmetic-rain questions were resolved and approved by the owner on 2026-08-25. No product decisions block planning.

## 1. Weather timing

Rain is continuous from client initialization until client cleanup. There are no random dry periods or server-synchronized weather phases.

## 2. Gameplay impact

Rain is cosmetic only. It causes no damage, slipping, flooding, visibility rule, scoring change, or round-state change.

## 3. Minimum visual scope

The first slice provides visible falling rain that stays around the local player's view. It deliberately excludes puddles, surface splashes, wet materials, shelter detection, lightning, thunder, and audio.

## 4. Readability and performance judgment

Density and appearance use conservative graybox defaults. The owner judges in Studio whether rain is recognizable, does not obscure the safe zone/platform/HUD, and performs acceptably before approving the feature.
